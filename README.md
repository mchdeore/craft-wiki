# CRAFT

CRAFT is a place for a small group to keep what it knows and work on it together: documents, tables, notebooks, files and conversations. Each is an object in one library. Every change is a version, and the library itself decides who may see what.

You use it in a browser. Someone who looks after CRAFT makes your account; there is no sign-up.

## At a glance

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| What can I put in it? | Texts, Word, Excel, PowerPoint and PDF files, tables, notebooks, conversations and skills. See [documents](documents.md), [tables](tables.md) and [notebooks](notebooks.md). |
| Who sees what I make? | You, until you share it. An admin sees everything. Where CRAFT is set up with hosted models, some of its text is sent to them: see [search](search.md#by-meaning) and [Sprout](sprout.md#what-leaves-craft). See also [sharing](spaces.md#sharing). |
| Can two people work on one thing? | Yes. Each edits a draft and commits it. Different pieces both land. See [commits](spaces.md#drafts-and-commits). |
| Can I get an old version back? | Yes, unless the object was removed for good or its early history was joined into one change. See [versions](spaces.md#versions-and-restore). |
| Can it run code? | Yes: Python in notebooks and in equations, in a small machine used once, with no network. See [notebooks](notebooks.md) and [machines](machines.md). |
| Is there an assistant? | Sprout. It reads what you may read and proposes changes that you make or decline. See [Sprout](sprout.md). |

To see it first, watch [the videos](videos.md).

## The screen

- The strip on the left opens a panel: **Files**, **Search**, **Notices**, **Jobs** and **Details**. Below them are Sprout, light or dark colours, and your name, which signs you out.
- What you open is a tab in the middle. Your open tabs and panels are kept in the library, so they are there in any browser you sign in to.
- The line at the bottom says what just happened. When the library refuses something, the line says why, in the library's own words.
